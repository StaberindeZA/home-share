# Home Share

Sharing a home isn't always easy, or at the very least can be made more fun.

Mono repo for services, sites, utilities that could come in handy when sharing a
home with someone.

## Definitions

Homes - Those 4 walls you have to yourself or are sharing with others. The most
common place where this app makes sense. In other apps, like Slack, these might
also be known as workspaces

Mates - G'day mate. Be it housemates, roomates, mate mates (friends),
partner-mates, or primates, it's the people that live in the Home together.
